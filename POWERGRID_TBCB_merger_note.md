**Subject: POWERGRID TBCB model — 17 SPVs were double-counted; corrected**

Hi,

MCA orders dated 27-Jan-2026 (appointed date 1-Apr-2024) merged 12 TBCB SPVs into POWERGRID Khavda II-C and 5 into POWERGRID Vataman. Our model still carried all 17 as separate forecast blocks alongside the two transferees, whose FY26 balance sheets were already restated as the merged entities — so Khavda II-C was running interest on 13 SPVs' debt against a single SPV's tariff, and its PAT turned negative from FY27.

We've blanked the 17 transferor blocks from FY24, restated both transferees to their filed merged accounts, corrected Khavda II-C's project cost (which was a plug) to the CEA estimate, and re-based depreciation and O&M on gross block. FY27 PAT is positive in both blocks. Project costs and tariffs tie to source.

Sources — the transferors are named in Note 51 of Khavda II-C's FY26 accounts and Note 49 of Vataman's:
