## Years where something broke or turned
- FY10: revenue -71% (9-month year plus loss of work); start of a four-year loss run.
- FY12: EBITDA turns negative (-Rs.6cr); stays negative to FY17.
- FY14: Rs.108cr capex year, 7.7x depreciation; the asset base that then had to be written down (gross block Rs.173cr in FY16 to Rs.95cr in FY17, part Ind AS deemed cost, part impairment).
- FY16: net worth Rs.10cr, net debt Rs.101cr. Near-insolvent.
- FY17: control change and Ind AS. Rs.89cr equity in, Rs.19cr liabilities written back (sits in other income Rs.29cr), Rs.43cr ESOP charge.
- FY20: peak margin 24%, PAT Rs.29cr, CFO Rs.89cr, net cash Rs.82cr.
- FY23: revenue -58%, PAT -Rs.44cr, receivables 362 days. The single most important year to explain.
- FY26: +70% revenue, mostly inorganic; EBITDA margin down to 12%; net cash of Rs.72cr at FY25 becomes net debt of Rs.11cr (gross debt Rs.158cr).

## What the numbers say before reading a single document
1. The company has made Rs.67cr of cumulative profit since FY09 and consumed roughly Rs.300cr of fresh equity and Rs.440cr of capex to do it. Cumulative FCFF since FY09 is negative. It is a capital consumer, not a compounder, so far.
2. Only one period (FY18-FY20) delivered profit, cash and returns together. The current growth phase has profit and returns but no cash: three-year CFO is negative while PAT is Rs.118cr, and receivables plus unbilled revenue stand at 209 days of sales.
3. Gross margin is meaningless here (ACE books no raw material); the cost structure is project expenses at 77% of revenue in FY26 (Rs.608cr), employees 5%, admin 5%. Operating leverage is in the project line, not fixed costs.
4. FY26 is a different company from FY25: 38% of revenue is now outside India (Kuiper), oil & gas is back to 80% of revenue, and gross debt went from Rs.24cr to Rs.158cr.
5. 1QFY27 (press release 13-Aug-2026, consolidated): revenue Rs.271cr (+135%), EBITDA Rs.21.9cr (8.1% margin, down from 12%), PAT Rs.12.8cr, order book Rs.1,754cr. Margin dilution from Kuiper is visible already.

## Questions only the filings can answer (carry to Stage 2)
1. FY23: which contracts ended and why did oil & gas revenue fall from Rs.189cr to Rs.63cr? Customer concentration at the time?
2. Receivables: who owes the Rs.348cr of debtors and Rs.210cr of unbilled at FY26, ageing, and what the Rs.22cr provision covers. Is the coal-CHP business structurally slow-paying (CIL subsidiaries)?
3. Kuiper: what was bought for Rs.123cr (contracts, tenure, margin, PAT contribution), why it carried Rs.72cr of cash, and how it was financed (Rs.97cr term debt drawn in FY26).
4. FY26 cost line "Other Mfg Exp" Rs.257cr (Rs.3.6cr in FY25): is this Kuiper manpower cost, and what is Kuiper's standalone EBITDA margin?
5. Exceptional items: FY21 Rs.12cr, FY23 Rs.6cr, FY24 Rs.-20cr/+19cr gross, FY26 Rs.9.4cr. What are they, and are any recurring in substance?
6. FY14 capex Rs.108cr: what was bought, what happened to it, and how much was impaired in FY16-FY17.
7. ESOP charges FY17 (Rs.43cr), FY18 (Rs.29cr), FY21 (Rs.30cr), FY26 (Rs.9cr): to whom, and total dilution.
8. Oilmax merger: swap ratio, assets coming in (E&P participating interests), related-party history with Oilmax (JV, Indrora field, allotments), and minority impact.
9. Order book Rs.1,754cr: split by segment, execution timeline, and how much is Kuiper.
10. Why did the company pay its first dividend in FY25 while raising equity and warrants in the same two years?
