## First questions the numbers raise (for the discussion)
- **Cash conversion is poor and getting worse.** FY26 CFO was Rs.6.2 cr against PAT of Rs.37.9 cr, and FCF was Rs.(1.2) cr. In the year receivables rose Rs.23.1 cr, inventories Rs.22.7 cr and other current assets Rs.10.4 cr. 9MFY26 CFO was Rs.(4.1) cr. Over FY23-FY26, cumulative PAT was Rs.80.4 cr, CFO Rs.67.4 cr and FCF Rs.29.1 cr. Inventory is 153 days of COGS (FY26), and the CFO said about 142 days on the 1QFY27 call. CARE puts gross current asset days at 215, up from 192.
- **ESG is shrinking and its plant is idle.** ESG (RUF/MNP, UN and tender business) revenue went from Rs.93.1 cr in FY24 to Rs.77.8 cr in FY25 and Rs.65.0 cr in FY26, and management put it at ~10% of 1QFY27 revenue. Thoothukudi RUTF utilisation fell from 68.7% (FY23) to 18.9% [M] in FY26, after capacity was doubled to 480 MT/month. HNIPL turnover fell from Rs.65.2 cr to Rs.52.6 cr, and it lost Rs.1.8 cr.
- **A few institutional buyers drive volatile revenue.** The top 10 customers were 42-49% of revenue. UN Agency 1 bought Rs.21.9 cr (FY23), Rs.42.4 cr (FY24), Rs.14.2 cr (FY25) and Rs.11.7 cr (9MFY26). In FY25 two customers (Foreign Distributor 1 and an Ethiopian government agency) made up 25% of revenue. ESG swung from 4% to 31% of revenue between years. There is no disclosure of receivable days by customer type. CARE blames the stretch on "longer collection periods for ESG products supplied to international institutional customers".
- **Earnings quality and the multiple.** Other income was 23% of FY26 PBT (Rs.11.9 cr), and FX gains were Rs.5.1 cr of 9MFY26 other income of Rs.8.0 cr. FY26 EBITDA margin of 13.7% only recovers the FY20-FY22 level (13-16%). 1QFY27 margin fell to 11.3% from 13.8%, with gross margin down more than 3 pts per management. At Rs.61.58 the stock trades at 19.1x TTM P/E, 25x excluding other income, and 13.5x EV/EBITDA.
- **Subsidiaries are now a drag, funded by the parent.** Consolidated PAT was below standalone in FY26 (Rs.(0.2) cr) and in 1QFY27 (Rs.(1.8) cr). The South Africa and Uzbekistan subsidiaries have a combined negative net worth of Rs.12.2 cr. Parent loans to wholly owned subsidiaries rose from Rs.28.8 cr to Rs.49.0 cr in FY26. Uzbekistan booked FY26 turnover of Rs.11.8 cr and PAT of Rs.2.8 cr while its plant ran at 1.6-3.3% utilisation.
- **Partial promoter exit and a board dispute.** (One family branch sold out; Vikram, Nikhil and Anuradha Kelkar still hold 45.7%.) Founders Arun and Subhash Kelkar, with Aditya and Nutan Kelkar, hold nil after the offer, and promoter-group holding fell from 89.4% to 45.7% (Prospectus p.160). Subhash Kelkar (ED) and Aditya Kelkar (NED) resigned on 7-Jul-2026. Arun Kelkar's term as Chairman lapsed on 28-Sep-2026. The remaining promoters (38.4%) requisitioned an EGM, which re-appointed him on 28-Oct-2026 and was also asked to approve the MD's FY27 pay. The FY26 results were also re-filed after an NSE query (scan, duplicate UDIN, XBRL error).
- **The one full plant is being rebuilt.** Nashik dry premix ran at 113.9% in FY25 and 95.6% [M] in FY26. It is the only well-used line. It is now being demolished and rebuilt after the agricultural-land regularisation (notices since Dec-2020), at a capex of Rs.25-30 cr. Production moves to Chennai, which runs at about 30%. Separately, there is a Rs.1.05 cr customs demand on the erstwhile HNEPL (1-Sep-2026).

## Open questions
- Revenue, margin and receivable days by vertical. What credit terms do UN agencies, foreign government agencies and distributors get?
- The order book of about Rs.100 cr (1QFY27 call): how does it split by vertical, and what tenders lie behind it? Why did UN Agency 1 drop from Rs.42 cr to Rs.14 cr?
- Uzbekistan: how does a plant at 2-3% utilisation book Rs.11.8 cr of turnover and a profit? Is it trading, and what is the repatriation risk? What is the plan for the South Africa and Uzbekistan negative net worth?
- Why did ESG fall in FY25-FY26, and is Thoothukudi (480 MT/month) stranded?
- Nashik rebuild: timeline, lost output, and the cost of moving production to Chennai and Thoothukudi.
- The promoter dispute: what lay behind the founders' full exit and the chairman's lapsed term?
