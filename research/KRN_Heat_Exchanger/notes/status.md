## First questions the numbers raise (for the discussion)
- **Profits are not turning into cash.** CFO FY23-FY26: Rs.5.1 / 16.9 / 21.4 / -113.8 cr against PAT Rs.32.3 / 39.4 / 52.9 / 76.5 cr; cumulative CFO is negative over four years. 1HFY27 CFO -Rs.132.8 cr on PAT Rs.69.0 cr. The IPO (Rs.342 cr) and the QIP (Rs.350 cr) have funded the plant and the working capital; Rs.134 cr of QIP money went to raw-material vendors within four months.
- **Inventory and receivables have ballooned.** Year-end inventory 166 days of revenue (FY26) vs 81 (FY25); raw materials alone Rs.213 cr. KRN HVAC held Rs.198.9 cr of stock on Rs.218.9 cr of sales (476 days) and owed Rs.83.6 cr to MSME suppliers. Receivables 106 days (FY26), 125 days of TTM revenue at Sep-2026. Management blames BIS import rules and war-time stocking and promises improvement by March 2027, partly via factoring.
- **Accounting classification moved.** Rs.211.7 cr of FY26 cost became "purchase of stock-in-trade" (traded goods), including Rs.104 cr re-labelled a year after 2QFY26 was filed; "others" revenue (semi-finished, bought-out parts, scrap) rose to Rs.81.7 cr (13.6%). GST receivable Rs.61.5 cr at Mar-2026 and a GST order of Rs.4.95 cr + interest + penalty for ineligible input credit (FY26).
- **The new plant is the case, and it is smaller than "6x".** Coil capacity rises from 5.80 lakh to 13.02 lakh (2.2x); "6x" counts headers, sheet metal and refrigerator parts. FY26 utilisation of the new plant 17.8% (guided 20-25%); FY27 tracking ~50% on the revenue run-rate.
- **One customer sets the margin.** Daikin (31% of FY26 sales) passes metal through but fixes KRN's absolute margin; when copper rose ~30%, 2QFY27 EBITDA margin fell to 15.3%. EBITDA margin 19.9% FY23 -> 16.4% FY25 -> 18.7% FY26 -> 17.1% 1HFY27.
- **Management guidance runs ahead of delivery.** Missed: exports 30-35% of revenue by FY26 (actual 16.6%), new-plant utilisation 20-25% FY26 (17.8%), RIPS from 3QFY26 (not booked), margins "20-plus" (15.3% the next quarter). Met / ahead: FY27 revenue and exports run-rate. FY28 ~Rs.2,000 cr is ~20% above what 80% of the new plant can produce at FY27 revenue per unit, and ~70% of the D&B FY2030 India finned-tube market.
- **Money trail.** Promoters sold 10 lakh shares in an OFS on 9-Jun-2026, eight days after the QIP at Rs.1,060 (stock then in the BE trade-for-trade series, 19-May to 10-Aug-2026). CFO changed in May-2026 (Sonu Gupta re-designated GM Finance; Pawan Nawal CFO). Exchange fine Rs.82,000 (risk-management committee, Jun-2025 quarter). Monitoring report misdates the IPO. Relatives on payroll; a promoter-family member (Komal Yadav) is a whole-time director of KRN HVAC. No pledges; same statutory auditor (Keyur Shah & Co., a proprietorship) throughout.

## 11-Nov-2026: standalone (parent) margin deep-dive (investor asked)
New sheet `Standalone_vs_Sub` in KRN_Model_v2.xlsx (script scratchpad/scripts/add_standalone_krn.py).
- Parent EBITDA margin: 17.5% (1QFY25) -> 8.9% (4QFY26) -> 12.0% (2QFY27). Gross margin ~25% to 1QFY26, then 13-19%.
- Main cause is arithmetic, and it ties: parent sold Rs 174.5 cr of raw material to KRN HVAC at ~cost in FY26 and bought Rs 120.5 cr back (AR FY26 related-party note). Standalone + subsidiary revenue - consolidated = Rs 295.1 cr = intercompany Rs 295.0 cr (FY25: 16.8 vs 16.7). Excluding sales to the subsidiary, parent EBITDA margin is 16.8% FY25 vs 16.9% FY26. Management said the same on the 2QFY26 and 3QFY26 calls.
- Real point: parent plant is full (coils 453,573 FY25 vs 454,406 FY26). Parent EBITDA ~Rs 17-25 cr a quarter; 1HFY27 Rs 44.7 cr vs 1HFY26 Rs 49.4 cr (-10%, 2QFY26 was a one-off high Rs 29.6 cr). ALL group EBITDA growth is the subsidiary: implied subsidiary EBITDA -1.5 (1HFY26) -> 53.1 (1HFY27), 59% of group EBITDA in 2QFY27. Profit is concentrating in the entity with 476 inventory days and a 17.16% tax rate, with transfer prices set between the two.
- Tax: KRN HVAC taxed at 17.16% (s.115BAB). Prospectus printed p.154 lists the condition "begin manufacturing on or before 31-Mar-2024"; new plant commercial production Apr/May-2025; KRN HVAC FY24 revenue Rs 0.2 cr. Open question, not a finding: does the 15% rate rest on the FY24 start, and is it accepted?
- Margin quality: management attributes part of margin to inventory gains on rising copper (3QFY26 call) and says Daikin keeps KRN's rupee margin fixed when metal rises (2QFY27 call), so % margin falls when copper rises. 2QFY27 consolidated 15.3%: freight ~2 pts, material cost averaging ~2 pts, Daikin pricing, promotion (2QFY27 call). Implied subsidiary opex Rs 22.2 cr -> 35.0 cr QoQ.
- 2QFY26 standalone cost split reported three ways (original: all materials Rs 183.1 cr; 3QFY26 filing: 82.5 + 100.6, internally inconsistent with its own 9M column; 2QFY27 filing: 79.1 + 104.1). Image-checked. Weak controls signal.
- Base case risk: FY28E EBITDA margin 19.1% (gross margin 31%, RIPS incentive Rs 13.5 cr). Sensitivity: 17.5% -> target Rs 792 (-18%); 16.5% -> Rs 734 (-24%); 15.0% -> Rs 646 (-33%). Base not changed yet; for investor decision.
