## What drives the numbers

**Spectrum is the whole story in FY23 and FY24.** DoT allotted spectrum administratively under
the BSNL revival packages, and BSNL capitalised it under *Entry license fees*: ₹19,808 crore in
FY2022-23 (900/1800 MHz) and ₹10,253 crore in FY2023-24 (700 MHz ₹8,209 crore, 2100 MHz
₹1,492 crore, regularisation fee ₹552 crore). Strip spectrum out and capex is far smaller and
much flatter across all five years — roughly ₹2,200 crore of cash PPE capex in FY23 and
₹11,125 crore in FY24, against ₹815–7,199 crore in FY20–FY22.

**FY2020-21 cash capex looks anomalously low** (₹815 crore) against ₹1,991 crore of PPE
gross-block additions and ₹3,493 crore of CWIP additions in the same year. That is capital-
creditor timing, not an extraction error — the 22nd Annual Report reprints the identical
₹81,533 lakh comparative.

## Caveats

1. **BSNL prints no single "capital expenditure" total** in its financial statements. Two
   defensible measures are given above: cash paid for fixed assets (cash flow statement) and
   accrual additions to gross block (asset notes). They diverge because of creditor timing,
   non-cash additions, and spectrum allotted against equity infusion.

2. **The segment note's "Capital expenditure during the year" row is unreliable in the 24th
   Annual Report.** In the 20th–22nd reports that row exactly equals additions to the PPE gross
   block (FY20 ₹5,56,787 lakh; FY21 ₹1,99,141 lakh; FY22 ₹1,21,923 lakh). In the 24th report
   the FY23 row (₹5,65,863 lakh) instead reproduces that year's depreciation and amortisation
   figures line for line, and the FY24 row totals ₹2,63,71,771 lakh — larger than BSNL's entire
   segment asset base. Both are misprints, so the asset notes are used instead.

3. **FY2022-23 was read from the 24th Annual Report's comparative columns.** The 23rd Annual
   Report PDF stores its financial statements with subset fonts carrying no Unicode mapping, so
   its numbers cannot be reliably text-extracted. The comparatives in the 24th report are the
   same audited figures.

4. **Standalone, not consolidated.** Consolidated figures differ marginally (FY23 PPE/CWIP
   acquisition ₹2,22,862 lakh consolidated vs ₹2,19,789 lakh standalone).

5. **FY2024-25 is not included.** BSNL's 25th Annual Report is not reachable on bsnl.co.in from
   this environment and has no Internet Archive capture, so nothing was extracted for it. For
   reference only — and *not* from an annual report — PIB press release PRID 2131702
   (27 May 2025) reports FY25 capex of ₹26,022 crore, comprising ₹15,324 crore of
   equipment/towers and ₹10,698 crore of spectrum. Treat that as a government press figure, not
   an audited line item, and not comparable with the rows above.
